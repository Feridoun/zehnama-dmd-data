## Data assets (releases)

The `dmd_medications.json` assets attached to each release contain data
derived from the **NHS Dictionary of medicines and devices (dm+d)** published
by the **NHS Business Services Authority** and distributed via the
[Technology Reference data Update Distribution (TRUD)](https://isd.digital.nhs.uk/trud)
service under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

In line with TRUD licensing, end users of Zehnama acknowledge:

* Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
  Licence v3.0.
* dm+d is © Crown Copyright. The NHS BSA holds the rights to the source
  data; this repository merely re-publishes a derived JSON view.
* Users must comply with TRUD terms of use when consuming these assets.
